# Chapter 1 · Valley opening assets and sound

11 November 2026. This scene reuses the accepted [David–Saul asset and voice pipeline](saul-generated-voices.md). No paid service, downloaded replacement character or named-person voice imitation was used.

## Character and environment sources

- Youthful David, foreground soldiers and Goliath use the existing [Quaternius CC0-derived project pipeline](../implementation/q02-quaternius-rig-probe.md) and its original clothing, facial controls and authored gestures.
- The standing-soldier LOD is an original bake/decimation of the prepared adult character with newly authored shield and spear geometry. It has **3,416 triangles**, no skeleton and no animation tracks, and is 223,904 bytes. [Generation source](../../source-assets/characters/valley_soldier_lod.py) · [Verified manifest](valley-asset-manifest.json).
- Forty-eight distant soldiers share that low-detail mesh through GPU instancing. They use varied stance sway; they are not 48 fully animated rigs. Two foreground soldiers have skeletal idle, walk, listen and speaking gestures. The champion's shield-bearer uses the baked standing model.
- The scene's terrain surface, grain/weave textures, sky painting, canvas shelters, ropes/stakes, timber, jars, limestone, olive trees and brook are original procedural project work. They give a period-oriented camp composition, not a claim of archaeological or photorealistic reconstruction. The playable approach retains the existing ground-height surface; only distant ridges receive extra relief.

## Generated speech and rights check

The [official Kokoro model card](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) declares Apache-2.0 and describes production/commercial deployment. The [kokoro-onnx runtime](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx/blob/main/LICENSE) is MIT. These permissions were checked again before generation on 11 November; [local licence copies](licences/) are retained from the prior sample and shipped in the build.

The already-downloaded **Kokoro-82M v1.0** and supplied standard voice bank generated seven original lines locally. `bm_george` supplies narration and one distant call, `am_michael` supplies the sentinel and another call, and `am_puck` supplies David. [Exact text, settings, clip durations, hashes and model hashes](valley-voice-provenance.json) · [Generation script](../../tools/generate-valley-dialogue.py). The model/voice hashes match the accepted sample's licensed files. Only the resulting 24 kHz mono WAVs and timing cues ship, not the generation model or Python environment.

The narration's opposing hills, champion, forty days and David's provisions derive from [1 Samuel 17:1–22](https://www.bible.com/bible/1/1SA.17.1-50.kjv). The sentinel, David and camp-call lines are original game adaptation, not verbatim biblical speech or quotations from a modern translation. Their dramatic performance remains subject to owner review.

Mouth movement follows the actual waveform's amplitude and spectral bands at 30 Hz, combined with the existing facial shapes. It is audio-driven jaw/lip motion, not phoneme-level articulation or facial capture.

## Original effects and mixing

Wind, canvas rustle, gravel steps, equipment texture and sparse low percussion are generated by the project's own Web Audio code. There is no imported music track or third-party effects library. Narration is centred; character/camp voices and steps use positional panners. The listener follows the camera, ambience lowers under speech, and mute leaves captions and the animation clock running.

The AudioContext clock drives sequence timing. Pause suspends audio and freezes camera/poses/mouths. Restart cancels scheduled lines and begins a new opening. The owner enables sound through Begin Chapter 1; the page does not request microphone access.
