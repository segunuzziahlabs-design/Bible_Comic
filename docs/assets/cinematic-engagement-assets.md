# Cinematic engagement assets

13 November 2026. [Implementation plan](../implementation/cinematic-engagement-plan.md) · [Exact asset manifest](cinematic-engagement-manifest.json).

**Subsequent owner correction:** [Jesse's courtyard](jesse-courtyard-assets.md) supersedes the messenger-delivered errand and flock-site pickup below. Jesse now gives the instruction personally, and the provisions are collected from his courtyard bench. The remainder of this record describes the earlier frozen cinematic-engagement candidate.

The retained Quaternius CC0 rig/mesh pipeline supplies the character basis, with the project's original wardrobe, skin weights, face controls and equipment. This pass adds original performance clips; it does not import reference-video art or audio. [Existing rights/source record](saul-generated-voices.md).

- **David adventure variant:** 9,893 triangles, 1,388,116 bytes. Retains the teenage model, appearance controls, mouth morphs, carry/delivery and sling clips. Adds a 3.5-second tending performance with baked two-bone arm IK and two 0.75-second lateral crouch/dodge clips. World displacement comes from the tested player controller; the clips contain body anticipation/recovery rather than scripted automatic escape.
- **Goliath encounter variant:** 11,785 triangles, 1,165,612 bytes. Retains the speaking model, armour, rigged spear and fall. Adds three spear performances with different upper-body twist/emphasis; warning/active/recovery timing is mapped onto the appropriate clip by the existing battle renderer.
- **Source:** [adventure_performance.py](../../source-assets/characters/adventure_performance.py), using the existing editable David-camp and Goliath-speaking Blender sources. New editable blends are retained under ignored `build/cinematic-engagement/`; original GLBs/blends remain unchanged. No new dependency or toolchain was installed.
- **Pasture, sheep, props and path:** original [source geometry/animation](../../apps/playcanvas/src/world/shepherd-world.ts). Eight lightweight sheep graze, turn and walk with per-leg gait phases. Static grass, hills, trees and rocks use instancing. These are stylised procedural animals, not a newly commissioned realistic animal asset set.
- **Voice:** seven shepherd and three encounter recordings generated locally through the existing Kokoro-82M / kokoro-onnx pipeline. [Shepherd script](../../source-assets/audio/shepherd-lines.json), [encounter script](../../source-assets/audio/encounter-lines.json), [shepherd provenance](shepherd-voice-provenance.json), [encounter provenance](encounter-voice-provenance.json). Model and supplied voice-bank hashes match the previously checked Apache-2.0/MIT sample. The asset check verifies actual WAV hashes and silent mouth-envelope tails. No named actor was cloned, service provisioned or film recording used.
- **Ambience/music:** original Web Audio wind, bleat-like harmonic tones, bird chirps and restrained plucked-note accompaniment. Existing battle equipment/footstep/spear/impact synthesis remains. These are synthesized effects, not natural animal field recordings. All added effects share the prologue audio context's pause clock, with a separate gain that follows the shared sound preference.
- **Mouths:** final-waveform amplitude/spectral envelopes, not phoneme-aligned lip sync. Existing facial morphs and gestures are retained.

## Story basis

[1 Samuel 17:12–40, KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Samuel+17%3A12-40&version=KJV) provides the shepherd context, Jesse's provisions errand, the keeper, camp, challenge and preparation. The dialogue is an original paraphrase. The messenger's delivery of the instruction and the three defensive spear exchanges are creative staging explicitly authorised in the owner's brief, not claims of specified events in the passage. An adaptation note appears on the episode entry. The existing approved biblical victory/non-graphic ending and no-reward policy remain.
