# CTRL-07 — first-time player check

Use the final normal episode link in the [CTRL-07 handover](../implementation/ctrl07-episode-integration.md). A browser automation run is not a participant observation. This checklist is prepared; no human result is implied.

Ask someone who has not played or built the episode to begin a fresh normal run. Explain only that they play as David and should follow the story. Let them discover the controls. Record the device/browser and orientation, whether they finish, where they hesitate, accidental actions, unexpected failures and requests for help. Note any coaching explicitly.

Observe whether they can:

1. Recognise when control returns after the messenger and follow the pasture arrows.
2. Understand that arrival is automatic and that Speak with Jesse returns afterward.
3. Take the courtyard provisions, follow the road and deliver them at camp.
4. Follow the challenge, Saul/armour and automatic stone collection without guessing.
5. Aim during practice, understand a miss and recover.
6. Read the spear warning, evade in a chosen direction and use Retry Battle after defeat.
7. Distinguish ordinary Throw from Jump Shot, finish the encounter and reach the conclusion.
8. Reload confirmed progress and understand Replay starts a new run.

Afterward ask: What was confusing? Did the controls do what you expected? Did the story hold your attention? Which moment felt strongest or weakest? Would you play another episode, and why?

Record observed completion/time and comments separately from interpretations. Prioritise progression blockers and control confusion before cosmetic suggestions. Owner creative acceptance and participant feedback remain separate from automated engineering verification.
