## Status definitions

**Complete and verified** requires appropriate evidence. **Implemented but not verified** requires an implementation but missing runtime proof. **Partially implemented** requires demonstrable partial work. **Not started** means absent implementation. **Blocked by a dependency or decision** identifies an explicit impediment. **Requires clarification** applies to unresolved scope or acceptance. The original assessment had no gameplay implementation. Current rows below incorporate EV11–EV18; a proof-only mechanic is Partially implemented relative to the complete episode.

## Reconciliation and priority

1. **P0 delivery feasibility:** A03/A05 and the local A04 proof now exist. Real-phone testing and the G1 decision must precede episode expansion; desktop touch emulation is insufficient.
2. **P0 product coherence:** U3 settles the length, encounter direction, style, orientation and access/checkpoint baseline. Final script/objective/adaptation details and art sample remain open. Generic technical proof and the approved mechanics can proceed.
3. **P0 schedule:** U2 compresses implementation from the document schedule to ten weekdays. The [roadmap](development-roadmap.md) makes the staffing and asset assumptions explicit and protects subsequent validation.
4. **P0 completion/quality:** a short courtyard loop exists; route, battlefield, sling encounter, ending and episode checkpoints remain absent. Build the complete path before incidental content.
5. **Supporting services:** U3 excludes login and cloud saves. A separate framework/database is not required by the baseline; keep these outside the critical path.

The user-approved directions are recorded as resolved decisions in the register, while unresolved decisions remain open. This avoids quietly following either outdated desktop-first wording or the conflicting David NPC proposal.
