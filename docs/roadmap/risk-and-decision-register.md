## Change and release rules

The owner may choose to release only after reviewing a working candidate, required evidence and limitations. Unresolved device support, broken critical-path gameplay, missing rights or missing external validation cannot be hidden by reducing a completion percentage. A changed requirement updates scope, task dependencies, effort, dates and the release recommendation together. Proposed future commits, hosting or purchases still follow the owner's authorisation; nothing in this planning pack performs those actions.
