## Scene and player experience

David meets Saul in the Israelite camp. The player approaches and selects **Speak** once. A 30–45 second voiced exchange plays with mouth movement, facial reactions, gestures and intentional camera shots. The shot blends back to David and the next objective becomes **Inspect Saul’s armour**.

Use the youthful David already selected for the game. Saul needs an approved adult character asset and period wardrobe. Two nearby soldiers listen and react. Their attention and posture support the exchange. Victory cheering is staged after Goliath falls in the later victory sequence.

The scene adapts the audience with Saul in 1 Samuel 17:32–39. The dialogue below is a proposed paraphrase for review. [Biblical passage](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/1samuel/17)

3. Stage the camera beats, soldier responses and audio mix on the same pause-aware cue clock.

The review must show a character speaking visibly with a matching voice, a listener responding, purposeful camera composition and background activity. Check hand and prop contact, feet, clipping and consistent lighting at both camera distances. Record phone performance evidence separately from layout emulation.
