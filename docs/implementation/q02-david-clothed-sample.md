# Q02 clothed David walk sample

9 November 2026 · David's teal tunic, belt, satchel, sandals and short curls are built on the tested Quaternius rig. The exported character measures **9,819 triangles** including the outfit, within the 8,000–10,000 triangle target and 31% below the stock body's 14,318 triangles. The GLB uses one skinned surface, one opaque material and vertex colours. It weighs 418,900 bytes raw and 188,235 bytes gzip. [Measured export](evidence/q02-david-2026-11-09/result.json)

The material uses a Godot companion resource with **Vertex Color > Use As Albedo** enabled. The installed importer retained the GLB colour data but left that flag off, which reproduced the Godot 4.7 import issue. Keep the companion material with the sample when opening it in the pinned engine. [Godot issue](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/issues/120625) · [Companion material](../../build/q02-david/godot/david-palette.tres)

The editable source retains separate wardrobe pieces. The generator trims the covered body, preserves the proven bone hierarchy, fits the cloth weights and merges the exported surfaces. The palette, clothing and hair were authored for the sample. Body and walk attribution follow the prior rig probe, including the provenance limit on the original base ZIP.
