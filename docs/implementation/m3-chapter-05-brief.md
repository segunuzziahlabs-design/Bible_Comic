## Technical boundaries

Retain PlayCanvas + TypeScript + Vite, the ordered `m2-outline-1` content/save contract and browser-local development adapter. Extend the existing movement/battle area through explicit optional chapter presentation hooks; defaults retain the earlier courtyard and Elah fixture. Put chapter orchestration, dialogue, authored camera shots and save UI in dedicated modules. Reuse the speaking David GLB, Goliath speaking GLB, instanced soldier ranks and established period environment. No new downloaded character art, paid provider or production deployment.

Normal pages must not seed preparation. Preview isolation is explicit. A confirmed save write precedes advancing to the review gate or retrying the battle. A failed write pauses the action and offers retry/reload. Restart cancels scheduled dialogue and clears unfinished throws without granting story credit. Visibility loss pauses the cinematic and fight.

## Acceptance and evidence

- Chapter 4's actual final practice hit unlocks Face Goliath and carries the same run/revision/inventory into the duel.
- Both speakers are audible and visibly animated in actual browser captures. Controls are unavailable during the exchange, then enabled once at its end.
- A miss leaves the attempt active. A telegraphed spear can be evaded; only its lethal hit records defeat. Retry restores battle entry, five stones and earlier progress, with no stale projectile or audio.
- A real forehead impact saves the eleven-objective prefix once, shows the fall, then stops before Chapter 6. Reload does not require another win or silently finish the episode.
- Production TypeScript/build and relevant rules/regressions pass. Touch layouts exercise actual input and fit essential 48-pixel controls. Emulation does not establish physical-phone GPU/Safari performance.
- Record measured startup/frame pacing and actual limitations. Owner reviews the voiced exchange, framing, scale, control handoff, combat and result before further chapter production.
