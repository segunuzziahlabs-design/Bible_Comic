# M3 · Chapter 1: Valley of Elah production brief

11 November 2026. The owner authorised Chapter 1 production, using the accepted David–Saul scene as the presentation reference, and requested a browser review before Chapter 2 production.

Implementation, playable preview and measured evidence: [Chapter 1 handover](m3-chapter-01.md). After browser delivery the owner instructed proceeding; [Chapter 2 production](m3-chapter-02-brief.md) is now authorised. The scope below records the original boundary and does not imply final release acceptance.

## Build order and scope

1. Retain the youthful David, licensed rig pipeline, episode objectives and safe checkpoints.
2. Produce a dedicated Chapter 1 scene: dry limestone valley, opposing camp ridges, canvas shelters, equipment, vegetation and a readable overlook route. Reuse the existing terrain collision surface; keep later chapter environments and combat unchanged.
3. Author a 36-second opening: David arrives, the valley is revealed, a nearby soldier speaks, Goliath is glimpsed across the brook, soldiers react and David resolves to find his brothers. Blend into player control.
4. Add two foreground soldier performances and inexpensive distant ranks, canvas movement, original ambience/footsteps/equipment/percussion, licensed generated narration and short character lines, mouth movement and readable captions.
5. Let the player walk to the overlook and choose Survey once. A short voiced survey completes only `valley.surveyed`, saves its checkpoint and ends at the Chapter 1 review gate. Do not produce or automatically play Chapter 2.
6. Test the exported browser build, including audio/motion pause, mute with captions, restart, interrupted cue ownership, actual movement, objective gating, saved handoff and portrait/landscape layout. Capture representative shots and measure this scene's frame pacing.

## Shot proposal

| Opening time | Shot and performance |
| --- | --- |
| 0–6 s | Low arrival shot, David's steps and camp canvas in the breeze. |
| 6–12 s | Slow wide reveal of the opposing ridges and ranks; narrated context. |
| 12–18 s | Soldier medium close-up, speaking mouth and restrained hand gesture toward the path. |
| 18–25 s | Goliath and the opposing camp across the brook; narrator establishes the champion. |
| 25–28 s | A nearby soldier turns, checks the line and reacts. |
| 28–32 s | David's close-up and short line, with a purposeful nod. |
| 32–36 s | Ease into the follow camera, close the subtitle and restore movement. |

Narrative context derives from 1 Samuel 17:1–3, 16–22. Dialogue is original game adaptation, not a quotation from a modern translation. Specific voices, timing and staging are reviewable creative work under this authorisation. Use the already licensed local Kokoro pipeline and supplied synthetic voices; record text, IDs and hashes. No new paid service or unverified downloaded art is needed.

## Review gate

The opening must visibly improve environment composition, camera presentation, speaking performance, soldier activity and sound. One coherent clock must hold camera, audio, poses and mouth timing through pause/resume. The player must regain control, reach the overlook and complete only Chapter 1. Review mode must preserve the player's existing episode progress. Present the local playable build and actual checks, then wait for owner feedback before Chapter 2 production.
