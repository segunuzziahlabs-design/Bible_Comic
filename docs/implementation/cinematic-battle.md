## Implemented encounter

The existing walking entrance, voiced confrontation, teenage/customizable David, character controller, follow camera, six chapters and account save interface remain. Only Chapter 5 opts into the extended encounter; earlier Elah fixtures and Chapter 4 practice retain their mechanics. There is no dependency upgrade or database/schema/content-version change.

1. **Sword.** Goliath walks towards David, draws the authored sword, then raises it over a locked strike location. A 2.3-second warning and marked ground precede the 0.44-second strike. Movement and directional Dodge remain under player control. Collision sweeps the actual animated blade markers against David's moving capsule, including his airborne height. Standing in its path can kill David; moving clear allows the stage to continue. The follow-through, ground dust and weapon sounds use the retained presentation system. One real evasion completes this stage.
2. **Spear.** Goliath restores roughly 9.5 units of separation and throws at David's position locked at the warning. The brief establishing angle ends during the warning; control remains available. The existing ballistic spear, rendered head/shaft, swept collision and physical landing remain. A dotted ground direction and landing circle explain the incoming attack. Two successful evasions unlock the sling; pressing Dodge alone does not grant credit or immunity.
3. **Sling.** David can reposition and aim before release. **Position** reports actual distance and facing angle: move closer, move back, adjust position or good throwing position. **Aim** independently reports higher/lower, towards the forehead, unsuitable angle or a predicted forehead alignment. The forehead ring identifies the target; reusable guide beads preview the physical arc. Neither guide changes a projectile's direction.
4. **Earned finish.** Only a genuine swept stone/forehead collision starts the labelled **cinematic replay**. Existing takeoff/air/landing clips and the sling layer show the dramatic action. The camera follows the recorded successful ballistic path in slow motion, frames the forehead impact and authored reaction, then uses the retained fall and Victory handoff. This is not a second gameplay stone, an automatic winning throw, or a way to convert a miss into a hit.

Stones launch at a fixed gameplay speed of 13.5 units/s with gravity 9.81 units/s². Swept sphere contact catches forehead intersections between updates; inaccurate directions can miss above, below or beside Goliath, and distant shots fall short. Terrain/armour impacts count as misses. The 5.5–12.5-unit positioning band is advice, not an invisible winning coordinate or a rule rejecting otherwise physical contacts. No retargeting or automatic gravity compensation is applied at release. The exact ballistic solver in the **test driver** moves the ordinary mouse/touch reticle; it is not part of gameplay.

F/click/Throw uses the current aim. Q/Jump Shot retains a physical player-requested jump and throw; it grants no invulnerability or guaranteed hit. Space remains normal Jump; Shift/touch Dodge uses the held movement direction. Without a held direction, the existing indicated quick-step direction remains available. The earned finishing replay is distinct from that optional player action.

## Failure, recovery and persistence

A lethal sword or spear contact retains `david_killed_by_goliath`, the existing defeat command and **Retry Battle**. Earlier chapters and all five-stone preparation survive. Retry clears attacks/projectiles/replay state, restores the battle inventory and requires sword and spear stages again. It does not reset the entire episode. An unfinished-battle reload resumes the battle checkpoint; transient combat stages are deliberately not added to the persisted schema.

Misses leave David controllable and do not confirm `goliath.struck`. If all stones are spent, the existing brook guidance/refill avoids a progression soft lock. The verified hit plays its replay first; entering the fall confirms the existing objective through the existing canonical save adapter. Save retry/error handling and Chapter 6 remain intact. A reloaded defeat uses neutral wording when the unsaved weapon detail is no longer available.

## Limits and reproduction

No critical progression blocker was observed in the executed cases. Collision uses bounded animated-weapon sweeps and conservative capsule/sphere volumes rather than per-triangle cloth/skin physics. Responsive checks use Chromium mouse/keyboard and actual CDP touch events; physical-phone GPU performance and Safari are not established. The current art style, subjective combat balance and performance/release budgets still need owner/M5 review. The finishing jump is explicitly a replay performance after a confirmed hit. A mid-battle reload starts the battle again rather than restoring an in-flight attack.
