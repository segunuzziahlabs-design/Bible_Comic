# David and Goliath episode design

The player acts as David in a **10–15 minute first playthrough**. David begins with a familiar responsibility in Bethlehem and ends by facing Goliath in the Valley of Elah. His trust in God gives the story its meaning. The player learns how to move, carries out the errand, prepares with the tools David knows, then aims and releases the sling. The approved direction calls for a fixed outcome, a non-graphic ending and illustrated 3D. The production blueprint maps these beats to missions with durable checkpoints. The pacing, dialogue and staging below are proposed for content review. | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| **Bethlehem** | 1.5–2.5 min | Learn movement and look in Jesse's yard. Press **Speak** once, then **Take provisions** when control returns. Walk to the gate. | Jesse sends David to take supplies and bring back news of his brothers. Reuse the staged exchange as the opening draft. The household cutaway, including Mattan, is adaptation material. **1 Samuel 17:12–20** |
| **Road and camp arrival** | 1–1.5 min | Follow a short route and leave the bundle at the camp supplies point. | The opposing ridges come into view. The route compresses travel and gives the player a landmark to follow. **17:19–22** |
| **The challenge and Saul** | 2.5–3.5 min | Reach the overlook and speak with Eliab. After both beats, meet Saul, try a few steps in his armour and set it aside. | Goliath's challenge explains the camp's fear. David tells Saul why he trusts God and chooses the equipment he knows. **17:23–39** |
| **The brook** | 1.5–2 min | Follow a nearby path to the wadi. Gather five stones in one interaction and see David put them into his pouch. | The staff, sling and pouch establish David's preparation before he enters the field. **17:40** |
| **The encounter** | 2.5–4 min | Approach the marked field entrance, listen to the brief exchange and take control of the aim. Adjust the guide and deliberately press **Throw**. A miss keeps the fight active. If Goliath kills David, choose **Retry Battle** to start from the battlefield entrance. | Goliath advances and taunts David. David answers in the name of the Lord. A successful sling release leads to the impact and fall. **17:41–50** |
| **The retreat** | 1–1.5 min | Watch the short conclusion, then choose **Play again** or return to the title. | The camera shows the Philistines retreating. A closing line establishes that Goliath died and David prevailed. Proposed treatment for verse 51 is to leave the sword action offscreen. **17:50–54** |

## Play and presentation

- **Exploration and preparation.** Use short paths, readable landmarks and contextual actions. The player can see the camp's objective from arrival and the brook's landmark after Saul. The compact camp, connecting route, grouped stone pickup and retries are game adaptations. Use the Iron Age setting as art direction and credit the adaptation.
- **Conversations.** Jesse's one-button exchange plays automatically. Retain its current 30-second cue as the first parity reference, then tune for reading and pacing. Short authored cues also carry the challenge, Saul's exchange and the duel's opening. Show speaker-labelled subtitles and actionable objectives with audio muted. Pause preserves the current line. Skip completes an eligible story cue once. Replay lets the player revisit the presentation without awarding progress again. [Current Jesse cue](../../assets/story/jesse_errand.tres)
- **The sling and retry.** Use an over-the-shoulder view with a visible aim guide. Mouse or touch drag adjusts aim, with keyboard aim and a reachable Throw action. A valid hit comes from the player's release. A missed throw leaves David in the fight. Only Goliath killing David triggers the defeat screen. Show the defeat without graphic detail and let **Retry Battle** reset the encounter from the battlefield entrance with the stones and preparation intact. The completed story retains its fixed ending.
- **Ending.** Cut from Goliath's fall to the opposing ranks and use narration to make the outcome clear. Use original adaptation dialogue for the story cues. The ending treatment and any direct scripture quotation need content review before final recording or art. [Biblical account and notes](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/1samuel/17)

Keep the speaking cast centred on **David, Jesse, Eliab, Saul and Goliath**. Mattan and the existing village activity can carry forward as opening material. Use small groups and distant silhouettes to establish the armies. The owner review should settle the exact dialogue, the offscreen treatment of verse 51 and the staging of Goliath's advance. Tune aiming tolerance and the length of the approach from the playable prototype.

Portray David as a boy of about 15. Players can choose his skin tone, hairstyle, hair colour and tunic colour. Keep his teenage proportions and shepherd wardrobe consistent across the options. The PlayCanvas preview lets players see their choices at full-figure and conversation distances.
