## First-time participant tasks

Give these tasks without explaining the route or controls. Let the game supply its own guidance. Record any help requested; intervene only when needed, and mark that task as assisted.

1. Begin David's story and follow what the game asks until you meet Saul. Explain in your own words why David came to the camp.
2. Continue Preparation, collect the stones and demonstrate a practice throw.
3. Attempt the battle. Explain the attack warning, positioning/aiming guidance and how to retry after defeat.
4. If time permits, complete the battle and conclusion, then find Replay Episode.

Allow approximately 15–20 minutes. A time limit is an observation, not an automatic game failure. Use headphones/speakers at a comfortable level; do not coach the first attempt.
