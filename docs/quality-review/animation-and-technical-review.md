## What the Lagos Life benchmark tells us

The [captured public map](evidence/2026-11-09/09-lagos-life-public-map.jpg) is itself stylised and economical: recognisable building silhouettes, colour-separated land/water/roads, varied landmarks, consistent labels and a designed interface. Clicking Market gives contextual feedback through a focused panel and softened background. That supports a concrete comparison with our flatter, more repetitive village and generic full-screen conversation card. It does **not** establish a character-animation comparison or prove equivalent performance: map-scale objects conceal detail that our close third-person camera exposes.

**Recommendation inferred from the comparison:** pursue cohesive art direction, readable places, purposeful character behaviour and transitions that acknowledge player actions. Those qualities make interactions feel like part of a world. More polygons or a different engine alone will not produce them. Do not copy Lagos Life's modern setting, account system, economy or map interface into this episode. The official [screen guide](https://lagoslife.app/wiki/screen) describes additional game systems; documentation is not evidence that they were played in this review.

## Stack and technical foundation

| Component | Assessment and bounded action |
|---|---|
| Godot 4 + GDScript | **Retain.** The exported sample works, and the existing components and checks are reusable. Godot supports blend spaces, state machines, filtered overlays, time scaling and one-shot animations through [AnimationTree](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/animation/animation_tree.html). Apply these around the imported AnimationPlayer; avoid competing playback owners. No evidence identifies GDScript as the bottleneck. |
| Blender → GLB → Godot | **Retain; improve asset authoring.** Reopened source retains all three actions. Export uses metre scale and sampled animation; runtime imports at 30 FPS. Sparse procedural keys and rigid weighting are the artistic limitation, not proof of an exporter failure. Establish clip naming, rest pose, loop flags, root-motion policy, sockets and reimport tests. Separate reusable AnimationLibrary resources from character wrappers. Retargeting requires compatible bone rests/mapping, not just matching names ([Godot retargeting](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/assets_pipeline/retargeting_3d_skeletons.html)). |
| Renderer | **Keep Compatibility for web.** Godot 4.7 web uses WebGL 2 and WebAssembly; Forward+/Mobile rendering and WebGPU are not available for this export path ([web guidance](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/export/exporting_for_web.html)). “Mobile renderer” is not the setting for a mobile browser. Budget for illustrated lighting rather than desktop-only effects. |
| Lighting and materials | A limited sun shadow experiment or inexpensive mesh-based contact shadow can improve grounding. Merely enabling the sun is insufficient while generated modules disable casting. Baked lightmaps are a later option: Compatibility can display them, but baking needs appropriate native hardware and UV2; current generated village meshes have no UV/UV2 channels. Saving bakeable geometry and adding UV2 is real pipeline work, not a checkbox ([renderer features](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/rendering/renderers.html), [LightmapGI](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/3d/global_illumination/using_lightmap_gi.html)). |
| Image quality | Test 2× MSAA against the disabled baseline; it improves geometry edges without blur but adds cost ([antialiasing](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/3d/3d_antialiasing.html)). Do not promise desktop post-processing, TAA or depth-of-field as the browser solution. Preserve UI readability independently of 3D scale. |
| Web delivery and services | Keep the existing small HTML/CSS/JS loader and single-thread export. No React, authentication, database or cloud persistence is needed for this approved scope. No such services were found. HTTPS, MIME/compression and cache/version handling still need a production-host decision. Respect the current laptop-only session decision; no tunnel/deployment was created. |
| Architecture and maintenance | Player, input, camera, interaction and village modules are separated. Preserve them. The proof controller still combines dialogue, story transitions, UI, sound and lifecycle; extract a narrow conversation sequencer before extending it. Keep story completion owned by the story state, not duplicated across animation callbacks. Runtime-generated static geometry complicates baking and visual editing; replace only modules that need an authored pipeline. Git is an unborn `master` with untracked files, and no CI directory exists: establish a versioned baseline when authorised, not an automatic commit now. |
| Developer tooling | The documented bare `python` command resolves to the Windows Store alias on this host and failed. Verification succeeded using the bundled Python executable. Document a consistent interpreter/launcher before handover; this is a tooling portability issue, not a browser-game failure. |
| Assets and rights | Current proof assets are locally generated; no third-party model/mocap was found. [Provenance](../assets/asset-register.md) is recorded, but final production designs and distribution licence remain open. For replacement assets/motions, record source, permitted game/web distribution, modification and attribution before adoption. Reusing licensed motion may save time but requires retargeting and visual cleanup; no paid tools/assets are approved by this review. |

An engine migration has **no demonstrated benefit yet**. A JavaScript 3D engine might permit a smaller tailored runtime, but would require porting controllers, collision, UI, story/animation integration and browser tests; it would not supply better assets and would still face device/GPU limits. Reconsider only after a representative polished scene fails agreed browser/performance gates and a bounded comparison measures a meaningful advantage. No replacement stack or migration estimate is justified from the present evidence.

### Performance findings and remaining uncertainty

The review HUD showed approximately **31–33 FPS**, including default-scale captures. These are sampled on-screen readings, not a sustained benchmark or GPU profile. The earlier Chrome report recorded version **156.0.8078.80**; the in-app browser's exact version was not collected. Previously recorded inspection hardware is Intel N150/Intel Graphics, approximately 15.8 GiB RAM, Windows 11; this does not establish the proposed 4 GB laptop/2 GB phone baseline.

The [export manifest](../implementation/evidence/b06-2026-11-09/build-manifest.json) records 39,514,754 raw / 10,263,606 gzip bytes for WASM, versus 140,288 raw / 100,176 gzip for the PCK. The engine dominates current transfer. Triangle reduction alone cannot materially fix that loading cost. Village geometry is only 9,196 triangles in 40 surfaces before actors/UI; this is not proof of headroom for realistic assets or shadows.

**No CPU-versus-GPU bottleneck has been isolated.** Profile a fixed route with diagnostics enabled/disabled, default/Extra-light resolution and shadows/AA individually. Record frame-time distribution, long stalls, CPU/script/physics time, draw calls and available memory measures. The existing `frame_ms` is Godot process time, not a GPU measurement or a complete browser presentation interval. Test compressed cold/warm loading on a stated network. Investigate a smaller custom engine template only if measured download budgets require it; this adds build/maintenance work. Physical-device thermal, memory, orientation and Safari evidence remains mandatory under the [lightweight plan](../implementation/low-end-device-performance-plan.md).

## Historical and story direction

Use the late eleventh/early tenth century BCE as the approximate David-era reference, rather than 500 BCE; traditional chronologies place his reign around 1000–970 BCE, with historical dating subject to debate ([chronology](https://pressbooks.ccconline.org/his111/chapter/1-4-the-hebrews/)). The current village is explicitly a placeholder, not a validated reconstruction.

Prepare a short reference sheet for highland settlement scale, construction, clothing, food containers, tools and vegetation. Review the current arched gateway, coloured window panels, regular street and striped stall rather than declaring them authentic or anachronistic without evidence. Rough stone foundations, mudbrick/plaster, timber, woven textiles and earthenware are useful research directions. Harvard's [house reconstruction](https://hmane.harvard.edu/house) is **late Iron Age**, so it is a materials reference, not an exact David-period model. Final historical/art direction still needs owner/content-review agreement.

The owner's automatic-conversation request supersedes the manual-advance direction in the [earlier story draft](../story/david-and-goliath-story-proposal.md). Reconcile that draft during the next implementation plan. A proposed first scene is: **Speak once → align David/Jesse → frame both actors → Jesse explains with gestures/subtitles → David acknowledges → set the provisions objective once → restore player control.** The script, shot timing, voice choice and artwork remain proposals. Voice-over is not required to prove the sequence and should not block it.

## Gate before expanding the story

Recommend acceptance of one exported David–Jesse quality sample against these criteria:

1. Owner accepts distinct characters and coherent period-informed art at actual desktop and phone viewing sizes.
2. Start, walk, turn, stop, idle and required gestures look intentional; no obvious skating, broken joints, perpetual speaking gestures or abrupt loop resets in recorded review. Run is excluded unless separately selected.
3. One interaction starts the acted exchange. Subtitles remain readable; automatic advance does not demand repeated confirmation. Pause, skip, replay and focus loss cannot duplicate objectives or strand controls. Pickup visibly matches the object/state change.
4. Three completion/restart cycles on the new export produce no progression blocker. Existing input, camera-collision and interaction regression remains passing after changes.
5. Confirm the existing proposed performance threshold with the owner: aim for 60 FPS where feasible; proposed floor 30 FPS on agreed devices, with rolling one-second FPS below 30 for over two consecutive seconds investigated as failure. The mobile floor, device models and download/memory/load-time budgets remain **open decisions**, not passed targets. Measure at least a 15-minute representative device run and tab return; log stalls and heating.
6. At least one new player completes this sample unaided and can explain the errand. Record completion/time/hints and answers to “What were you meant to do?”, “What felt confusing or unnatural?” and “Would you continue, and why?” This is a proposed pilot gate, not statistical evidence for the full episode or replacement of the later user study.

**Recommendation:** continue engineering through Q01–Q06, withhold additional story/environment production until the quality sample is accepted. Begin with Q01's concrete David–Jesse visual/animation and shot plan, then replace the shared placeholder characters and motions. Do not begin by changing engines or simply raising resolution.
