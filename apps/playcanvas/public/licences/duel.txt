DAVID AND GOLIATH - CHAPTER 5: THE DUEL

Five original dialogue paraphrases were generated locally with the previously
licensed Kokoro-82M v1.0 weights (Apache-2.0), standard am_puck, am_fenrir and
bm_george voices and kokoro-onnx 0.6.1 (MIT). Model and runtime notices accompany
this file. No voice cloning, performer imitation or reference-film audio.

Existing David, Goliath and soldier assets derive from the recorded Quaternius
CC0 pipeline, with original project clothing, equipment and animation. The
Elah terrain, sky, vegetation, camp dressing and synthesized effects are reused
project work. No new external character, music or sound pack was downloaded.

Story context: 1 Samuel 17:41-50, adapted into original game dialogue.
Spear telegraph, assisted aiming and brook refill are game adaptations.
No reward is granted. Only Goliath's lethal strike causes defeat.
