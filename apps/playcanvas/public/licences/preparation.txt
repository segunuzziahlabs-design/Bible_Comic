DAVID AND GOLIATH - CHAPTER 4: PREPARATION

The accepted three David-Saul recordings are reused. Eight additional original
lines were generated locally using Kokoro-82M v1.0 (Apache-2.0), standard
am_puck and am_michael voices, and kokoro-onnx 0.6.1 (MIT).
Model/runtime notices accompany this file. No voice cloning, named-performer
imitation, reference-film audio, external sound pack or paid provider was used.

David and Saul derive from the existing Quaternius CC0 character pipeline.
The loaned armour and its stand props, fitting/removal, armoured trial
walk treatment, stone pickup and the brook/target geometry and textures are
original project work. Existing characters and accepted assets are retained.
Wind, water, movement, equipment and impact effects are original synthesis.

Story context: 1 Samuel 17:32-40, in original game paraphrases. Sling practice
at a woven target is a game adaptation. No reward or new defeat rule is added.
