DAVID AND GOLIATH - CHAPTER 2: DAVID ARRIVES

Original game dialogue generated locally using Kokoro-82M v1.0 (Apache-2.0)
and the supplied standard am_puck, bm_george and am_michael voices through
kokoro-onnx 0.6.1 (MIT). Model and runtime notices accompany this file.
No voice cloning or named-performer imitation was used.

Models derive from the existing Quaternius CC0 character pipeline, with
original project wardrobe, facial controls and performances. The teenage
David's new carry/set-down action and the camp's procedural props, textures
and synthesized environmental effects are original project work.
Background figures reuse the existing standing-soldier bake.

Exact scripts, model/voice and asset hashes, authoring sources and rights
records are preserved in docs/assets/camp-voice-provenance.json,
docs/assets/camp-asset-manifest.json and docs/assets/camp-arrival-assets.md
in the source project. Dialogue is an original adaptation of the provisions
and greeting context of 1 Samuel 17:17-22, not a copied modern translation.
